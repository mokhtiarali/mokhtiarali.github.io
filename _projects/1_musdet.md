**Code:** [Multimodal-Intelligence-Lab/MuSDeT](https://github.com/Multimodal-Intelligence-Lab/MuSDeT) · **Paper:** [CVF Open Access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026W/MULA2026/html/Ali_Multi-Scale_Encoding_Gated_Fusion_and_Temporal_Context_for_Lightweight_Wearable_CVPRW_2026_paper.html) · **Poster:** [PDF](/assets/pdf/musdet_poster.pdf)

We propose a lightweight architecture for stress detection from wearable biosignals (EDA, HR, temperature, etc.) that combines multi-scale temporal encoding, gated cross-modal fusion, and a short-range temporal-context module. The design targets on-device inference on consumer wearables while maintaining competitive accuracy against heavier transformer-based baselines.
